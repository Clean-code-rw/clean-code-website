# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Clean Code RW is a community where developers of every background and experience level can learn and build together. We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new to programming or to open source
- Giving constructive feedback on the code, not on the person who wrote it
- Accepting feedback gracefully and learning from mistakes
- Respecting different opinions, experiences and ways of working
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Insulting, demeaning or discriminatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Harassment, in public or in private
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions, and in public spaces where someone is representing the project or the community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the Clean Code RW maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on how serious the behaviour is, they may issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently ban the person from the project.
